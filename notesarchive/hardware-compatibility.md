2 buttons for model selection with 8 bi colored leds

total of 24 synthesis models

4 knobs for 
- Frequency
- Harmonics
- Timbre
- Morph

3 knobs for attenuverters (timbre, FM and Morph)

---

Simple Touch has:

- 8 pots: 
  - 2 Faders + 6 knobs
- 2 toggle switches (on/off/on)
- 12 Touchpads
  - P10 - P11 (top) usefull for toggles
  - P0 - P2 (middle) due to P1 being tucked between the switches, this is harder to reach. P0 and P2 are usefull to be used as e.g. a minus and plus button, or toggles/ triggers 
  - P3 - P9 (bottom) (most logical for entering e.g. notes or set triggers)
1 LED (userled) can only be toggled on/off, no fading

There are options to connect extra things like: oled screen, leds, clock . Clock is the easiest as you could just connect a mono jack and not worry, but for real CV it's dangerous to connect high voltage sources.
